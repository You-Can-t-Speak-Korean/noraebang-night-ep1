## 1. Act Overview

### Pitch
1979. 장보람 is seven, in her first year of 국민학교, the day after the President's head was blown off on live melody. Nobody cares. Over six years she survives a spoiled rich girl, a teacher who loves the rod, a PTA mother with a shard of glass, and an anti-communist with the most communist name in Korea.

### The spine: one intrusive question per boss
Each boss attacks a different part of 보람's identity.

| # | Year | Grade | Boss | The question |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1979 | 1학년 | 김은주 | 너 어디서 왔어? |
| 2 | 1980–81 | 2–3학년 | 정정훈 선생님 | 도시락에 뭐 싸왔어? |
| 3 | 1982 | 4학년 | 현정 엄마 | 아빠는 뭐 하시니? |
| 4 | 1983–84 | 5–6학년 | 홍대중 | 너희 아빠 나라 빨갱이야? |
| — | Feb 1985 | 졸업 | Ending | ❓ |

### Act flow
```
Opening (assassination → title → classroom)
  → Boss 1 loop → year-change TV montage
  → Boss 2 loop → year-change TV montage
  → Boss 3 loop → year-change TV montage
  → Boss 4 loop (Act final)
  → 1985 ending
```

**Boss loop:** Dialogue scene 1 → (Dialogue scene 2) → Noraebang boss fight → Aftermath beat.

## 4. Language Curriculum (TOPIK ~2) 🟡

### Act-wide targets
- **반말 vs 존댓말:** who gets which, and getting it wrong.
- **Sentence endings:** -아/어, -아/어요, -습니다/-ㅂ니다.
- **Tense:** present, past (-았/었-), future (-(으)ㄹ 거예요).
- **Requests and prohibitions:** -(으)세요, -지 마세요, -아/어 주세요.
- **Question words:** 어디, 누구, 뭐, 왜, 언제, 어떻게, 몇.
- **호칭:** 선생님, 아줌마, 아저씨, 언니, 누나, 형, 오빠, "현정 엄마" vs "현정이 어머니".
- **의성어·의태어:** ideal for kids' songs and attacks (쾅, 퍽, 쨍그랑, 활활, 뚝뚝, 펑).
- **School-institution vocabulary:** 조회, 종례, 주번, 반장, 숙제, 도시락, 운동회, 학예회.

### Per-boss focus
| Boss | Language focus |
|---|---|
| 김은주 | 반말 basics; question words; party vocabulary; 의성어 |
| 정정훈 | -요 and -습니다 to a teacher; classroom commands; food vocabulary from the 도시락 |
| 현정 엄마 | 호칭; family and job vocabulary; polite visitor phrases |
| 홍대중 | Simple past tense (the essay is a story); set phrases; 신고하다, 간첩, 나라 |

### Era slang 🔍
Early-80s children's expressions, to be researched and collected into the era slang codex.

## 5. Boss Loops

### Boss 1 — 김은주 (1979, 1학년)

**Dialogue scenes 🟡**
- **Scene 1: The morning after (27 October 1979).** Picks up straight from the opening. 은주 corners 보람: "너 어디서 왔어?"
  - Example choices: deflect ("서울에서 왔어."), overshare ("우리 아빠는 에티오피아 사람이야." → privacy meter hit), counter ("너는?").
- **Scene 2: The invitation trap.** 은주 hands out birthday invitations to the whole class — and makes a show of giving 보람 hers last. Brags about her family's **color TV** 🔍 (color broadcasting didn't start until December 1980, so the rich family owns a color set showing black-and-white — a running gag).

**Arena:** 은주's birthday party, around the family's home **반주기**.
**Song:** 〈생일 축하해, 나만〉 — bright 80s 동요-pop.
**Language:** 반말, question words, party 의성어.

**Attacks 🟡**
- Birthday candles + hairspray flamethrower
- Shaken 사이다 bottle fired as a glass rocket (parody brand)
- Party poppers packed with thumbtacks
- Party-hat cone driven into the eye
- Booger flick escalating to booger sniper ✅
- 종이인형 paper-doll blade storm
- 반주기 mic cord: strangling, then electrocution ✅
- Cake knife throw
- **Final chorus:** the family's brand-new color TV dropped from above

---

### Boss 2 — 정정훈 선생님 (1980–81, 2–3학년)

**Dialogue scenes 🟡**
- **Scene 1: 도시락 inspection.** The 도시락 tower warms on the classroom **조개탄 stove**. 정정훈 checks for mixed grain 🔍 and sniffs 보람's lunch: "이게 뭐야? 냄새가 이상해." Players must use correct 존댓말 ("네, 선생님. …입니다.").
- **Scene 2: 학예회 casting.** He casts 보람 in the school play as "the foreigner," or "the African," and refuses to hear objections. A chance to resist politely — or not.

**Background TV:** color broadcasting begins (Dec 1980) 🔍; Seoul wins the Olympic bid (Sep 1981) 🔍.

**Arena:** the **학예회 stage**.
**Song:** 〈사랑의 매〉 — trot. ("The rod of love," the era's euphemism for corporal punishment.)
**Language:** -요 and -습니다, classroom commands, food vocabulary.

**Attacks 🟡**
- Chalk machine-gun — 백묵 lodged in the forehead
- 출석부 guillotine
- 당구 큐대 rod (era-authentic) that splinters into a spear
- Slipper slap at bone-shattering velocity
- 엎드려뻗쳐 freeze — then he stands on her back
- Red-hot 양은 도시락 tower hurled from the 조개탄 stove
- Flying 조개탄 coals
- Thrown chalk and 출석부 smacks
- **Final chorus:** 사랑의 매 frenzy — the rod of love goes full chainsaw

---

### Boss 3 — 현정 엄마 (1982, 4학년)

**Dialogue scenes 🟡**
- **Scene 1: Parent observation day (참관수업).** 현정 엄마 stands at the back of the class with the other mothers, then catches 보람 in the hallway: "현정이 친구니? 엄마는 뭐 하시니? 아빠는?" A 호칭 test — and a privacy test.
- **Scene 2: 운동회.** She runs the 육성회 tent, rearranges the relay teams, and announces that 보람 can't run because "외국 애들은 다리가 길어서 반칙이야." 현정 is caught in the middle.

**Background TV:** curfew lifted (Jan 1982) 🔍; 프로야구 launches (1982) 🔍 — kids in parody team jackets.

**Arena:** the **운동회** stage and field.
**Song:** 〈치맛바람〉 — old 가요 ballad.
**Language:** 호칭, family and job vocabulary, polite phrases.

**Attacks 🟡**
- Glass shard from a shattered 운동회 trophy case — throat gash and blood everywhere on a wrong word ✅
- 줄다리기 rope noose; rope-burn decapitation
- 박 터뜨리기 — the basket bursts and drops knives instead of confetti
- 오재미 packed with rocks at bullet speed
- Relay baton impalement
- Bladed 양산 parasol
- Thermos of scalding 보리차
- 만국기 bunting garrote
- **Final chorus:** 운동회 megaphone at eardrum-bursting volume

---

### Boss 4 — 홍대중 (1983–84, 5–6학년) · Act final boss

**Dialogue scenes 🟡**
- **Scene 1: The poster contest.** The 반공 poster and essay contest is announced. 홍대중 inspects entries and stops at 보람's: "에티오피아? 거기 공산주의 아니야?" 🔍 (Ethiopia was ruled by the Marxist Derg regime from 1974.) The irony: her father fought *against* communists in the Korean War.
- **Scene 2: The essay reading.** 보람 reads her essay aloud. 홍대중 decides she is a spy's daughter and reaches for the phone to dial **113** 🔍.

**Background TV:** 1983 is peak 반공 fever 🔍 — KAL 007 shot down, the Rangoon bombing, and the 이산가족 찾기 broadcasts.

**Arena:** the **반공 contest stage**.
**Song:** 〈간첩 신고는 113〉 — military march.
**Language:** simple past tense, set phrases, 신고하다, 간첩, 나라.

**Attacks 🟡**
- 반공 poster spear ✅
- Crayon shiv
- Rotary phone dialing 113, used as a bludgeon
- 삐라 paper-cut blizzard 🔍 (kids collected North Korean leaflets for prizes)
- 민방위 air-raid siren as a sonic attack
- 등화관제 blackout — she must answer by sound alone
- Medal-pin impalement
- **Final chorus:** the stage floor opens into a spike-lined North Korean 땅굴

## 6. Boss Fight Design

### Attack design principles ✅
1. **Every weapon comes from the scene and the era.** No generic weapons.
2. **Escalation follows the song.** Verse is nasty → chorus is worse → bridge breaks the rules → final chorus is pure absurdist carnage.
3. **Wrong word:** the hit lands, in full gore.
4. **Right word:** the attack is reflected, dodged or turned back on the boss.
5. **Extreme enough to be ridiculous.** The target reaction is "OMG" and "WTF," then laughter.

### Phase structure 🟡
| Phase | Song section | Blank difficulty | Attack intensity |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verse | Easiest | Nasty |
| 2 | Chorus | Easy | Worse |
| 3 | Bridge | Moderate | Rule-breaking (e.g. blackout, freeze) |
| 4 | Final chorus | Hardest of the fight | Signature carnage |

### Counters ❓
- (a) **Gory reflection:** the boss gets the same treatment.
- (b) **Slapstick reversal:** cartoon-satisfying, less bloody.
- (c) **Mixed:** slapstick through the fight, gory reflection on the final chorus.

### Game over ✅
- The news anchor reports 장보람's gruesome death in the same flat tone as the assassination.
- Classmates trade 딱지 over the broadcast. Nobody cares.
- 🟡 Variations: a unique headline per boss; a running "death count" ticker across the Act.

## 9. The 1985 Ending ❓ (open for brainstorming)

Direction ✅: built from (a), (b), (d) and/or (e).

- **(a) The lottery.** Middle schools are assigned by lottery (뺑뺑이) 🔍. 보람 draws her school. So does 김은주. Same one.
- **(b) Graduation 짜장면.** The traditional graduation-day 짜장면 at a 중국집. Callback to 〈그 새우, 그 짜장〉: the radio plays 진두통's comeback single, and nobody cares.
- **(d) The class photo.** The graduation album arrives. 보람 has been cropped out, replaced, or labelled wrong.
- **(e) A combination**, e.g. (b) at the table → (d) when the album is passed around → (a) as the final sting.

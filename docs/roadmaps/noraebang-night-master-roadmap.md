## 1. Vision & Pillars

### Elevator pitch
A satirical horror-survival language game. A Korean-Ethiopian woman fights her way through fifty years of Korean hierarchy — from grade school in 1979 to the presidency in 2030 — one Noraebang Night at a time.

### Pillars
1. **Language is survival.** Every fight and every social scene is won or lost through Korean. The player never answers a question just to proceed; the Korean *does* something.
2. **눈치 is a combat stat.** Reading subtext, register and the room matters as much as vocabulary.
3. **Unapologetic satire.** 갑질, hierarchy, judgment, 회식 culture, chaebols, politics and religion are all targets.
4. **Historical specificity.** Every era looks, sounds and *speaks* like itself.
5. **Violence lives in the noraebang.** Outside it, the brutality is social.
6. **No sexual content. Ever.** The one line the game does not cross.

### Target players
- **Primary:** advanced learners of Korean (upper-intermediate and above).
- **Secondary:** Korean players and the Korean diaspora who will play it for the satire and recognition.

### What makes it distinctive
- Language progression mirrors a Korean life: 반말 → 선후배 존댓말 → 하십시오체 → political rhetoric.
- **Era-specific slang across 50 years** — players learn how Korean itself changed from 1979 to 2030.
- A protagonist whose outsider-insider position makes every intrusive question a gameplay moment.

## 5. Core Systems

### 5.1 Dialogue & 눈치 system
- Choose the correct speech level and 호칭.
- Read subtext ("편하게 해" ≠ relax).
- Deflect without offending.
- Timed responses in high-pressure moments.

### 5.2 Privacy & Integrity meter 🟡
- Tracks how much private information she gives away and how far she compromises herself.
- Carries across all four stages.
- Affects available dialogue, boss difficulty and the **ending**.

### 5.3 Noraebang boss fights
- Each boss picks a song in its signature genre.
- The player completes lyrics through fill-in-the-blank choices while the boss attacks.
- Correct lyrics **counter, dodge or reflect** attacks; wrong ones land the hit.
- Boss phases escalate as the song progresses (verse → chorus → bridge → final chorus).
- Every boss has a unique attack set and a fitting song genre.

### 5.4 Era layer
Smoke density, noraebang technology, prices, menus, fashion, slang, phones and background news change by year.

### 5.5 Progression
Language mastery unlocks stronger lyric counters and better dialogue options. Optional **era slang codex** collects expressions across 50 years.

## 9. Release & Business

- **Self-published:** ❓ Steam, itch.io, or both.
- **Pricing:** ❓ per episode, season pass, or both.
- **Content warning screen:** informs, does not apologize. Often required by storefronts.
- **Marketing:** lean into the controversy; Korean community channels; language-learner communities; streamers.
- **Legal:** pre-release review of the satirical names. Korean defamation law considers whether a person is *identifiable*, so a one-time review protects the game from takedowns rather than censoring it.

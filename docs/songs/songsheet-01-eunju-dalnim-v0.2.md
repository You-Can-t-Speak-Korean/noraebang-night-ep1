| 10 | Final | 달이 다시 ___ 한 입에 꿀꺽! | 뜨면 / 뜨지만 / 떴어 | **뜨면** | Condition -(으)면 | The family's color TV dropped from above | 🩸 The TV bounces off the 반주기 and lands on 은주; sparks fry her "sunny side up" |

🤡 = slapstick counter · 🩸 = gory counter (Systems Spec §5.3: verse and chorus lean slapstick, bridge and final chorus lean gory)

### Distractor notes
- **#1** 파란 / 까만: grammatical, but a yolk is yellow.
- **#2** 냉장고 / 숟가락: 커다란 fits, but the next line's 기름 and frying need a pan.
- **#3** 멍멍 (dog) / 엉엉 (crying): real 의성어, wrong sound for oil.
- **#4** 고추장 / 김치: the 하얀 clue rules out red foods.
- **#5** 누구를: doubles the object with 달을. 언제: clashes with 밤마다.
- **#6** 있어요 / 많아요: the song's logic is that the moon is eaten by morning.
- **#7** 봐요 / 볼 거예요: 어젯밤 requires past tense.
- **#8** 밝아요 / 따뜻해요: the yolk has burst; the sky is dark.
- **#9** 먹었어요: grammatical, but she is still hungry and plans to eat it when it rises again. 먹지 마세요: a -세요 command to herself. 🔍 Native review: confirm 먹었어요 is clearly wrong in context.
- **#10** 뜨지만: "rises, but" breaks the logic. 떴어: breaks the sentence.

## 5. Fight Outcomes

### 🏆 Revenge win — no wrong answers, all 3 hearts
*(눈치 cross-outs allowed; a 눈치 dodge counts as a mistake)*

The 반주기 plays the 100점 fanfare. 은주 lunges for the mic to claim the score, trips on her own cord, and face-plants into the cake. The candles ignite her hairspray. She staggers into the 장식장; the 양주 bottles rain down and go up like fireworks. The color TV topples last and lands on her with a crunch. On its cracked screen, the black-and-white news keeps playing. Through the window, the moon hangs perfectly round and yellow.

The guests keep eating cake.

### 😐 Survival win — at least 1 heart left, with mistakes
The 반주기 shorts out and the party ends early. 은주 sulks into the cake: "흥. 다음엔 안 불러." She never bullies 보람 again. She just pretends she doesn't exist.

### 💀 Game over — 0 hearts
The news anchor, in the same flat tone as the assassination:
> *"오늘 저녁, 국민학교 1학년 장보람 양이 생일 파티에서 사망했습니다. 다음 소식입니다."*

Back in the classroom, the kids trade 딱지. Retry.

🟡 The headline names the attack that landed the final hit (e.g. "…컬러 TV에 깔려 사망했습니다").
